# Trademark policy

"DAG Brain", the DAG Brain logo (the three-node mark in `frontend/index.html` and `docs/assets/`), and the name "SlightlyHappy" identify the official project created and maintained by SlightlyHappy. The code is open source under the GNU AGPL v3; the names and logo are not. Section 7(e) of the license, as set out in [NOTICE](NOTICE), excludes them from the license grant.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name "DAG Brain" to refer to the official project, for example "we analysed our graph with DAG Brain", in papers, talks, posts and teaching.
- Say that your own project is "based on DAG Brain" or "a fork of DAG Brain", as long as your project has its own distinct name and logo and does not suggest that it is official or endorsed.
- Run unmodified copies of the official releases for yourself, your lab or your organisation, under the official name.

## You need written permission to

- Distribute or host a **modified** version under the name "DAG Brain" or with the DAG Brain logo.
- Use the name or logo in a product name, company name, domain name or app-store listing.
- Imply that SlightlyHappy endorses, sponsors or maintains your version.

## Forks and rebranding

You may fork the code under the AGPL. A fork that is distributed or offered to users over a network must:

1. use a different name and logo;
2. keep the attribution "DAG Brain, created by SlightlyHappy" with a link to https://github.com/SlightlyHappy/DAG_Brain in its About screen or equivalent legal notices (see [NOTICE](NOTICE));
3. keep every copyright header in the source files;
4. publish its complete source code under the AGPL v3 to every user, including users of a hosted version.

Permission requests: open an issue at https://github.com/SlightlyHappy/DAG_Brain/issues titled "Trademark request".
